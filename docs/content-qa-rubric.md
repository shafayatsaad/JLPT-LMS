# Content QA rubric

Every published question and form must pass the checks below. Schema validation and item counts are necessary, not sufficient.

## Question review

- Japanese is correct, natural, and appropriate for the assigned level.
- The question measures the assigned official item type.
- Exactly one answer is defensible.
- Distractors are plausible and clearly incorrect.
- The answer key, explanation, and distractor rationales agree.
- The item and its passage/dialogue are original and copyright-safe.
- Duplicate and near-duplicate checks pass.
- Tags, timed section, score category, assets, and shared-stimulus grouping are correct.
- Listening audio matches the reviewed script and is quality-checked.
- A reviewer records a decision, revision, and notes; approved revisions are immutable.

## Form review

- The form matches a versioned level blueprint and current official section timing.
- Only eligible question revisions and approved assets are included.
- Shared passages/dialogues remain together and cannot be split incorrectly.
- Counts, item-type quotas, difficulty distribution, tags, and duplicate constraints pass.
- The form has a deterministic assembly seed and manifest and is frozen before publication.
- No answer key or explanation is exposed before completion.
- Grading is deterministic and idempotent; results use raw platform performance labels.
